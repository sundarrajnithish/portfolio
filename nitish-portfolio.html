About

An engineer who started in sales — and it shows.

I'm currently a Specialist in Power Application Engineering at Schneider Electric, providing end-to-end energy management and critical infrastructure monitoring for commercial and industrial customers across Eastern Canada. Most of my work sits at the IT/OT boundary — supporting data centers, industrial operations, healthcare facilities, and large real estate portfolios where power visibility isn't optional.

Before engineering, I spent two years as an outside sales representative and several months in customer service — time that taught me how to read a client's actual problem, not just their stated request. That instinct carries directly into how I scope and deliver EPMS solutions today: technically sound, but built around what the customer needs to trust the system.

I hold a Master of Engineering in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Concordia University and a Bachelor's in Mechatronics, Robotics, and Automation Engineering from SRMIST — a combination that lets me move comfortably between the electrical, control, and software layers of a facility.
